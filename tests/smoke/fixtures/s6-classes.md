The manager routing reference (the seven consequential-judgment classes; each
routes to advisor under the default):

1. initial nontrivial request triage, scope interpretation, and decomposition strategy
2. evaluation of significant plans and architecture choices
3. risky tradeoffs, competing approaches, and reviewer design challenges
4. reconciliation of inconsistent worker, verifier, reviewer, or Git evidence
5. recovery strategy after meaningful failures or blocked work
6. integration-readiness decisions on ambiguous evidence
7. substantive final synthesis and recommendations
